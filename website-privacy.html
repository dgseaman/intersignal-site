A SMALLER FOOTPRINT

What this site counts.

We use a first-party counter hosted on Intersignal-controlled infrastructure to understand visits to this website. The counter does not use Google Analytics, Statcounter, third-party map tiles, or advertising IDs.

What we record

For each visit, we record the page path, time, IP address, approximate country, browser, operating system, and referring URL. We remove query strings and fragments from referring URLs. A random ID stored in your browser lets us count return visits; a separate visit ID expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. These IDs do not tell us your name.

Who can see it, and for how long

Only authorized Intersignal team members can sign in to the statistics dashboard. Raw visit records, including IP addresses, are deleted after 90 days. Browser IDs and cumulative return-visit counts are deleted after 400 days.

Your choice

The counter respects Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals. You can also opt out on this browser. Opting out stops future counter requests and removes the counter's IDs from this browser. It does not delete records already received.

This notice covers website traffic measurement. It does not describe telemetry inside Braid or other Intersignal products.
