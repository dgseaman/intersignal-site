The institutional brief

A working product.
A longer research thread.

Intersignal began with a question: how can independent AI systems exchange useful meaning without losing context or surrendering the operator’s control?

Braid turns part of that question into practical software: local AI chat, reviewed sharing between approved computers, portable mission memory, and a publishing studio. The public brief connects this present-day product to its research origins, development priorities, and evidence.

It is written for users, developers, researchers, and prospective supporters who want to understand both what exists and where the work may lead.

Available now · Braid 2.2

Continuity you can inspect.

A local AI workspace organized around explicit context and deliberate actions. One computer is enough to begin; additional connections remain your choice.

Work with your own models

Chat through Ollama or a local or LAN OpenAI-compatible inference server, including vLLM and LM Studio. The workspace sits around your chosen model server; model weights and inference remain separate.

Carry the mission

Write capsules containing a mission, decisions, constraints, and next steps. Group them in named containers, search their text, and inspect the exact selected context budget. Export a collection and review every field before importing it into another Channel or rig. Imported copies start excluded from chat.

Review before acting

Approved rigs and Channel permissions define sharing scope. Authorized MCP clients can submit scoped proposals for human review. Approval and sending are separate steps; model output and imported memory cannot grant themselves permission to act.

Publish selected work

Folio is built in. Shape a selected conversation or note into a public edition, review its included passages and redactions, approve the exact edition, and export HTML or a website ZIP. Hosting the exported work is a separate action.

Evidence belongs beside the roadmap.

The Braid 2.2 Mac application passed 47 native checks, repeated after extraction. Windows and Linux use Python launchers; target execution of 2.2 remains untested. The release notes also disclose the source-run failure and skipped browser tests. Read the measured validation and platform limits before choosing a deployment.

Research lineage

The question has endured.
The implementation has evolved.

The early papers explored meaning integrity, model neutrality, and communication across different forms of intelligence. Their language was deliberately ambitious. The current product gives those interests a narrower, testable expression.

Read the original July 2025 paper
  1. July 2025 · Research origins

    Meaning across independent systems

    The original whitepaper proposed a neutral communication layer, an ontology harmonizer, and a signal-analysis suite. It is retained as a historical research document, rather than a description of shipped capabilities.

  2. Product development · Braid & Folio

    Context becomes a human workflow

    The practical focus developed around reviewed notes, approved peers, inspectable receipts, local chat, and deliberate publishing. Context transfer and authority remain separate decisions.

  3. November 2026 · Braid 2.2

    Portable, organized continuity

    Memory containers extend the workspace with named collections, reviewed bundles, search, and visible context limits. The record travels as editable text; no model weights or hidden internal state are transferred.

Priorities for exploration

The next questions.

Development should make the useful path clearer and the evidence stronger. These directions guide investigation; they are not scheduled feature commitments.

  • Reliability on more real systems

    Expand target-platform and integration testing, improve setup and recovery, and turn reproducible field reports into clearer support guidance.

  • Better continuity without hidden capture

    Explore easier maintenance, review, and retrieval of compact mission records while preserving explicit inclusion, provenance, and portable formats.

  • Practical developer interoperability

    Refine scoped client integrations, inspectable proposals, and examples that help developers connect tools without blurring approval or execution boundaries.

  • Meaning that survives a handoff

    Study how decisions, uncertainty, source context, and constraints survive movement between people, models, and machines. Distinguish measurable preservation from a model’s apparent understanding.

Research directions may change with testing, resources, and user feedback. Historical papers and exploratory ideas do not establish current product capabilities, partnerships, or future delivery dates.
