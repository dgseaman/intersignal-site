# Start Braid Archangel 1.9.2

On macOS arm64, copy **Braid.app** into Applications and open **Braid**. The
application includes its runtime; Python installation and a terminal are not
part of the normal journey. This local build is ad-hoc signed and is not
Developer ID notarized. Gatekeeper acceptance is not claimed by the test report.

In Braid's left sidebar choose **Folio — Publish to Web**. Or open a Channel and
choose **Publish with Folio** on a completed conversation or a saved note.
Selected material becomes a private draft. Compose, review the public page and
exact JSON, explicitly approve, and export. You decide when and where to upload
the approved files.

Use **Back to Braid** to leave the studio. Use **Settings & tools → Quit
Archangel** to stop the application. Restart keeps your local work. Closing the
browser alone leaves the local application running.

An existing standard Folio profile is copied automatically once. If its old
standalone launcher is still running, close it and launch Braid again. Read
`MIGRATION_NOTES.md` before intentional profile changes or rollback.

Keep LAN/WAN transport, invitation acceptance, and rig-card approvals deliberate.
A public Folio page is not a permission to access your Braid workspace.

See `TEST_RESULTS.md` for release qualification and platform limits. Source
launchers and native Linux/Windows build recipes are developer entry points,
not additional apps to download or launch for normal Folio use.
