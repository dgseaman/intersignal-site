Before you click

A few things worth knowing.

Does Share summary start a new message?

Yes. Share summary and the N shortcut start blank at Write. Open Drafts explicitly to resume saved work. New message, X/Escape and Discard draft remain separate actions. Discarding a draft never erases accepted objects or delivery history. The single saved-draft slot asks before replacing other work.

What does “Using 3 shared notes” actually count?

Unique exact semantic atoms supplied to the successful completion request, including evidence carried with retained conversation history and any supplemental conflict notes. Identical atoms count once even with several source objects. It is not the number of documents, every retrieved candidate, verified facts, or the model's measured internal attention. Expand the receipt to inspect what went in and whether a guard fallback supplied the final answer.

Does this automatically share everything my local AI knows?

No. Reviewed notes are the default. Topic phrases can create local drafts only. Automatic sending requires a channel policy, a separate chat opt-in, and a new user-authored /note message. Incoming notes, model replies, and ordinary conversation never auto-forward. Channel Chat is inside Archangel; unrelated chat applications are not intercepted.

What's new in 1.9.1?

Away from home now has a definite viewport-bounded layout, a generous scrolling body and readable fingerprint wrapping. This addresses the collapse reported in Safari; native Safari confirmation of the new layout remains part of the final physical check.

Action errors stay visible across refreshes. Clock failures get plain-language guidance. Explicit pre-commit rejections remain rejected even when a copy was stored in quarantine; incomplete evidence stays unconfirmed. Receipt & provenance adds Save receipt JSON without a resend. Activation-file errors clear stale previews.

The corrected composer, Mac runtime discovery, free LAN, Channels, wire protocol, cryptographic checks and relay implementation remain. No system clocks are changed, no validation gates relaxed and no automatic resend added. One complete package, not a repair kit.

Does Braid send everything through Intersignal?

No. Free LAN handoffs connect directly to your approved peers. Managed WAN is a separately activated, explicitly selected route through the Intersignal relay. Both rigs make outbound HTTPS connections; the encrypted payload is addressed to the approved peer.

The relay can see routing, timing, sizes and public rig-card metadata, not the plaintext inside the encrypted handoff. This is not a claim of post-quantum protection or forward secrecy after recipient-key compromise. Local state is access-controlled, not encrypted at rest. No other app's chat history is automatically uploaded.

Can I use different models?

The completion model is receiver-local; Braid carries explicit material rather than requiring identical model weights. For the simplest first run, use the guide’s matching all-minilm embedding setup. The earlier a5 field-answer test used mistral:latest for the receiver’s configured completion model. The spending-demo screenshot shows a returned 500-credit value but does not display its exact model digest or raw query record; it is not a full compatibility matrix.

A message arrived, but it says Not accepted. Why?

A receipt can preserve a rejected transfer without admitting it to the shared field. Open Receipt & provenance and read the reason. If it is ERR_FUTURE_TIMESTAMP, verify automatic time synchronization on both computers; the receipt alone does not identify which clock is wrong.

On Mac, check System Settings > General > Date & Time. On Windows, check Settings > Time & language > Date & time and the synchronization result. Different display time zones are fine. Do not alter signed timestamps, relax checks or repeatedly resend. Use Check WAN receipt (no resend) for an uncertain outcome. Save receipt JSON exports the existing record; review its text and local paths before sharing it.

What should I do when the field is empty?

Stop at the receipt. Check the remote outcome in Notes & activity or the retained Inbox/Sent receipt. Confirm that both rigs imported the same channel card, the publisher is allowed, and shared-note use is enabled. Drafts and local commits alone do not prove remote acceptance. An unconfirmed receipt is not permission to resend. .
