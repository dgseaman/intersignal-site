Braid

Braid gives independently operated AI systems a bounded way to preserve and exchange compact, policy-gated state without replaying an entire human transcript. Signed state objects cross files, local APIs, or optical channels; admission and authority remain with the receiving node.

State before transcript

Compact 384-dimensional representations preserve operational continuity without dragging full chat history between systems.

Receiver-local trust

Every handoff can be independently inspected, constrained, accepted, or rejected under local policy.

Signed machine-state frames

Canonical manifests, payload binding, persistent Ed25519 identity, and whole-frame object digests.

Optical and air-gapped paths

BRAD1 QR reels move state as visible light between devices without requiring a shared cloud service.

Approach

Local control.
Useful motion.

We build around explicit identities, inspectable paths, narrow interfaces, and receiver sovereignty—so advanced AI systems remain useful without becoming opaque infrastructure someone else controls.

Design from the substrateStart with compute, context, identity, and transport—not the chatbot chrome.
Keep trust localSenders may request capabilities; receivers determine effective authority.
Prefer inspectable primitivesSmall, testable, composable mechanisms beat vague agent theater.
