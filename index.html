COGNITIVE COMPILATION

Traditional software models force you to transmit raw, human-language transcripts of your intellectual capital to corporate API gateways. This architectural compromise creates continuous operational risk and permanent data harvesting liabilities.

Our architecture pivots. We treat the local edge node as a private cognitive crucible—a "Blender." Rather than uploading files or chats, the local node ingests all multi-modal streams (keyboard text, voice audio, camera feeds, and secure documents), blending them locally through optimized local reasoning models.

"We do not chase trends. We build primitives. Intelligence doesn't need to reside in commercial servers; it is compiled, compressed, and synchronized locally at the physical network layer on your own terms."
— David Seaman, Operator of Intersignal

Once blended, these multi-modal states are projected into dense 384-dimensional latent vectors. These compact numerical representations are cryptographically signed and synchronized over physical UDP subnets.

WAN Peering Topology

COGNITIVE ALIGNMENT SCHEMA NodeId-first WAN Routing Protocol // Tendril Kernel

Braid Tendril abstracts physical IP addresses completely. By implementing signed presence records and routing candidates directly in the **Tendril Transport Manager**, edge nodes achieve seamless peer synchronization across variable wide-area routes, gracefully falling back from direct QUIC/UDP paths to zero-trust opaque transit relays without breaking active Handshakes.
