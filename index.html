The choices stay yours

A few useful answers.

Does Braid share all my conversations?

No. You select and review the material. Each receiver controls its own permissions. Private Folio originals and excluded passages stay out of Braid networking and public exports.

What does Folio publish?

The exact edition you review and explicitly approve. Editing public content invalidates approval. Folio exports HTML or a site ZIP; you use your own hosting or platform account. Scheduled streams prepare review drafts, not automatic publications.

Can a reader continue with me on Braid?

You may add an invitation to a publication. A reader requests a connection; you decide whether to accept. Rig-card exchange, fingerprint approval, and scope controls remain explicit. A public page never grants Braid authority.

What happens to existing Braid and Folio work?

Quit the old app and back up your complete private profile before upgrading. Braid retains identities, Channels, grants, and history. A standard Folio 0.3.0 profile is copied once into separate integrated storage; the original remains. Legacy drafts need a fresh review. Read migration notes.

Which computers can use this download?

Braid-1.9.2.zip includes the self-contained Apple silicon Mac app and updated Windows/Linux launchers, just like the launcher approach in 1.9.1. Windows/Linux need standard CPython 3.10–3.13. Their launchers install pinned dependencies into Braid’s private runtime on first setup. Local chat uses your Ollama models; Folio opens inside Braid on every platform. Physical testing on Windows/Linux is still pending.
